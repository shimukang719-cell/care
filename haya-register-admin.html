Haya Community Care

A mother-and-son founded, nonprofit community volunteer initiative

100% free. No fees, no product sales, and no commercial profit.

Community Wellbeing: We aim to support older adults living alone, people with disabilities, and low-income residents in Ireland through free muscle-relaxation and fascia-relief activities, where suitable qualifications, training, insurance and safeguards are in place.

Nature & Wildlife Care: We care for the local environment, wild birds, and small wild animals.

Kindness towards people. Compassion for nature. Rooted in Ireland, volunteering for our community.
