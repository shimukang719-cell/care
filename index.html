About Us

Caring for people, wildlife and our shared home.

yymkcare.com is a completely free, nonprofit community care platform, jointly established and independently operated by a mother and son. With no commercial fees, advertising revenue or intermediary charges, it offers permanently free services for local residents, people facing disadvantage, and micro, small and medium-sized businesses.

Four ways we care

01 · Community care

Free everyday support for older people, people with disabilities and those with limited mobility: home haircuts, parcel collection, nail care and basic household cleaning and tidying.

02 · Nature & wildlife

Documenting urban ecology, sharing wildlife care and low-carbon living, and promoting knowledge and care for the local environment and small urban animals.

03 · Free local information

Planned permanently free job, job-seeking and rental listings, connecting local residents, jobseekers and businesses through transparent information. This section is under development.

04 · Small business support

Free guidance on ERP systems, workflows and management practices for small businesses facing financial pressure, limited budgets or inconsistent processes.

All platform functions are intended to remain free, transparent and noncommercial. We focus on gaps in everyday support, unequal access to local information, environmental education and small business management.

Our Story

This website was jointly founded by a mother and son. The mother oversees the project vision, service planning and section design; the website’s technical development is undertaken independently by her son.

Technical Co-founder | Mukang Shi

Studying in the first year of senior secondary education at O’Connell Secondary School in Dublin 1, Ireland.

Alongside his schoolwork, Mukang takes responsibility for the website architecture, front-end and back-end development, programming, mobile layouts, deployment, debugging and day-to-day maintenance.

Through self-directed learning, he turns technical skills into practical community services, developing the website structure, booking interfaces and information services as the platform grows.

Vision & Operations Co-founder

Responsible for the platform’s direction, community care service design, service standards, business consultation content, external coordination and daily operations.

A shared idea, built together: the mother brings the service vision and operational planning; her son brings the technical work.

The team has no commercial investment, outsourced technical development or revenue model. Independent ideas and a young person’s technical skills bring together community care, nature protection, local information and business support.

Animal Care

Kindness extends to the animals that share our city. We promote respect for wild birds and small urban animals, care for their habitats and share everyday ways to protect nature.

Wild birds & urban animals

Observe wildlife respectfully, keep a comfortable distance and avoid disturbing nests or natural behaviour.

Protecting shared habitats

Encourage cleaner green spaces and waterways, less litter and responsible use of our shared outdoor spaces.

Nature education

Document local ecology and share wildlife knowledge, environmental awareness and low-carbon living ideas.

Support for Businesses in Difficulty

Free ERP & business management consultation

We offer free consultation for micro, small and medium-sized businesses under pressure: short on staff, facing management difficulties or unable to afford an adviser.

What we can help with

01 · ERP & stock workflows

Basic ERP operation, purchasing, sales and inventory workflows, and organising accounting documents.

02 · Everyday processes

Workflow improvements, consistent record keeping and document archiving.

03 · Costs & records

Guidance on cost calculations, income and expenditure records, and orderly business documentation.

04 · Simple management systems

Practical management procedures for small businesses and answers to everyday operational questions.

No fees. No sales pitches. No bundled purchases. No hidden charges.

We use practical experience to offer free technical and management support, helping businesses organise processes, reduce gaps and improve everyday operations.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Public community updates contain activity summaries and aggregate figures. Please do not publish private contact details or identifiable service-user information.

The registration and booking demonstration forms save data only in the current browser. Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Published community updates are stored in Firebase and visible to website visitors; draft updates are private to their authors and authorised administrators.

Contact hayakk@gmail.com about correction or removal of published personal information. A full privacy notice will be provided before real online registration or booking collection is enabled.
