Haya Community Care

A mother-and-son founded, nonprofit community volunteer initiative

100% free. No fees, no product sales, and no commercial profit.

Community Wellbeing: We offer free, practical community assistance where volunteers are available, including help with haircuts, collecting parcels, temporary safekeeping of belongings by prior arrangement, and other everyday tasks. All support is subject to availability and appropriate safeguards.

Nature & Wildlife Care: We care for the local environment, wild birds, and small wild animals.

Kindness towards people. Compassion for nature. Rooted in Ireland, volunteering for our community.
