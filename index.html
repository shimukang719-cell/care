About us

Care, Connection, Community

HAYA Community Care is a volunteer-led initiative that encourages social connection, kindness and practical neighbour-to-neighbour support in Dublin. Our aim is to make everyday life a little easier, especially for older adults and people who may feel isolated.

♡

Compassion

Friendly, respectful support that values each person's dignity and independence.

☘

Community

Encouraging local friendships, inclusion and a sense of belonging.

✦

Volunteering

Small practical acts of kindness, offered free when volunteers are available.

Free services

Everyday help and wellbeing

Our activities are non-medical and entirely free. Each request is considered individually, depending on volunteer availability and appropriate safeguards.

📦

Parcel Collection

Helping collect or receive ordinary parcels with the recipient's clear permission.

🛍️

Carrying Everyday Items

Helping carry manageable shopping bags, groceries and other everyday items.

🧺

Simple Errands

Assistance with straightforward local errands, where arrangements are safe and practical.

💬

Friendly Companionship

Conversation and social companionship for people who would appreciate company.

🌿

General Wellbeing

Encouraging positive routines, social connection and community participation without clinical advice.

🤝

Community Guidance

Sharing publicly available community information and signposting to appropriate organisations.

Scope of support: No medical diagnosis, treatment, massage, physical therapy, personal care, emergency response or professional transport. Volunteers do not handle cash, valuables, restricted goods or unsafe loads. Assistance requires consent and confirmation.

Privacy

Privacy and safety

This website's demonstration forms store information only in the visitor's own browser. They do not create an online account or transmit requests to HAYA. Please do not enter real personal data. Before introducing real registration or support requests, a secure system, appropriate privacy notice, and safeguarding arrangements will be required.
